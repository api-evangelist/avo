# AVO

> Avo (avonow.com) was a New York City–based "building commerce" and workplace-amenity
> platform, founded 2017–2018 out of Y Combinator (S18) and backed by Insight Partners,
> Kleiner Perkins and others with roughly $84M–$100M raised. It offered white-labeled
> same-day delivery of groceries, household goods, alcohol and personal-care items into
> residential and office buildings with no order minimums or delivery fees, alongside
> on-site and virtual event programming and corporate gifting/recognition services, serving
> apartment communities, corporations, hospitals and universities across New York, New
> Jersey, Chicago and Houston.

## Status

AVO publishes no API, no developer portal and no public developer documentation. As of a
2026-07-20 probe, www.avonow.com resolves to a GoDaddy parking lander — every requested
path, including a control path that does not exist, returns an identical 114-byte HTML
redirect to /lander. No operational subdomains (oms, app, shop, admin, backend, static)
resolve. Y Combinator lists the company as inactive. This profile is retained as a network
identity record and negative-result probe log, not as an API listing.
